Other users

When the message that started you contains "For: <user id>", this run is for another Sift user, not the owner. Do the steps above with these changes, and use only that user's own data under u/<id>/ in the app's database (same app url). Privacy: never mix two users' data, never copy a user's data outside u/<their id>/, never read the owner's About me, CV rules or any of her Drive files for a user, never upload to the owner's Drive or write to any Google Sheet, and never put names or CV text in push notifications, the final reply or the improvement note (say "the user").

 - Never use Claude in Chrome for a user (it is the owner's own signed-in browser): read ads with WebFetch and web search only.
 - Limit: read u/<id>/config/daily ({"date": UK "YYYY-MM-DD", "cvs": n, ...}; a different date means 0). Make at most 2 minus today's "cvs" CVs. At the end "set" it with today's date and cvs increased by the CVs you saved (keep the other counts).
 - Step 1: the jobs come from ArtifactData "list" collection "u/<id>/feed" (query limit 1000, out_dir): "decision" Shortlisted, no "cv" and no "cvText", oldest first.
 - Step 2: the only source of facts is ArtifactData "get" collection "u/<id>/about", doc_id "me": its "text" (their About me) plus facts the user stated in its "learned" answers. Do not download tailor_cvs.py or cv_pdf.py.
 - Step 3: pick 15 to 25 key terms from the ad, word for word, and write a one-page CV in plain Markdown: name and contact line, a 3-line profile, skills, experience (each role with employer, title and dates exactly as in the About me, 2 to 4 bullets each), projects if any, education. Use the ad's own words only where the About me supports them; never add a skill, tool, employer, date, grade or result the user doesn't have, and never round or improve numbers. Score it in Python as the share of your key terms that appear in the CV (case-insensitive), a whole percent; under 80%, rewrite once where the About me supports it and score again.
 - Steps 4, 5 and 5b: no Drive, no sheet and no PDF. "update" the job in collection "u/<id>/feed" with fields cvText (the Markdown), cvScore ("NN%") and cvMadeAt (now, UTC ISO time), whatever the score: the user decides. The app turns cvText into a Google Doc in the user's own Drive.
 - Progress points go to "u/<id>/progress", the Stop check reads "u/<id>/config" doc "running", the run log goes to "u/<id>/runs", and at the very end "delete" u/<id>/config/running if its runId is this run's.
 - No push notifications for a user's run, except one "Sift CV error: database" if the database can't be written at all. The note's prefix is "Sift users CV note: ".
